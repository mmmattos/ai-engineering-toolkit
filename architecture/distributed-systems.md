# Distributed Systems

> Design and review distributed systems with explicit treatment of failures, consistency, concurrency, and resilience.

# Purpose

Assume that networks fail, processes stop, messages are delayed or duplicated, and dependencies become unavailable.

Consider:

- failure domains
- partial failure
- latency
- timeouts
- retries and backoff
- idempotency
- consistency models
- concurrency
- leader election where relevant
- replication
- partition tolerance
- observability
- recovery

Make distributed-system trade-offs explicit rather than hiding them behind infrastructure abstractions.
